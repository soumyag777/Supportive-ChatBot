# Supportive-ChatBot
# ✿ Buddy Bot ✿

A supportive chatbot with a pet companion you can actually pet. Vibe coded and designed for mobile first.

🐱 **[Meet your buddy](https://soumyag777.github.io/Supportive-ChatBot/)**

## Features
- 🐾 **Choose a buddy:** Mochi the cat, Biscuit the pup, Clover the bunny, or Honey the bear
- 💕 **Tap to pet:** your buddy squishes, floats hearts, and says something sweet every 5 pets
- 💬 **Rule-based chat:** responds to sad, anxious, stressed, tired, lonely, and happy moods with comfort, breathing exercises, and affirmations
- 🧠 **Understands "not":** "not feeling good" gets comfort, not celebration
- 🛟 **Safety first:** serious messages point people to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- ⚡ **Quick reply chips** for fast, low-effort check-ins

## Built with
HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, split into `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Design notes
Petting gives users something to do with their hands when words are hard. Quick reply chips lower the effort of reaching out, and the crisis rule makes sure a cute bot never gets in the way of real help.
